## Platform

web

## Users

Decision-makers commissioning video: brand and marketing teams, government ministries and PSUs, and entertainment creators. They arrive to judge whether Palco can handle their brief, then send one.

## Product Purpose

A single landing page that explains what Palco Video does and who it serves, proves it with real work and clients, and gets a brief sent. Success means a qualified brief reaches a producer.

## Positioning

A legacy production house, built on Ranjit Studio and est. 1985, where production and marketing sit in the same house.

## Operating Context

Three offices (Delhi HQ, Chandigarh, Mumbai). A lead producer replies to a brief with crew availability, location feasibility and an indicative cost band, usually within two working days.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static single-file `index.html`, no build step.
- The contact and newsletter forms open a pre-filled email. There is no submission backend.
- No prices are published.

## Brand Commitments

The parent site, palcovideo.com, is the binding brand: its name, red accent, type families, film-production language and tone. The landing page must read as the same company.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real films and Shorts on YouTube, linked throughout the page.
- Named clients and stats exactly as published on palcovideo.com.
- Founder portraits and the archive photo in `assets/images/`.
- Absent, so never fabricate: testimonials, case-study results and pricing.
- Open: "200+ projects delivered" conflicts with other published figures and needs confirming by Palco. Whether the service photos are from real Palco shoots is unconfirmed.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work before describing it.
2. Every claim comes from Palco's published material.
3. Say plainly what Palco does, then prove it.
4. The page leads to one action: send the brief.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
