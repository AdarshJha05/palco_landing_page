[ Who we are ]

Who we are

Palco Video is the brand- and business-facing arm of a legacy production house. For four decades the studio has made films, serials and Gurbani recordings for broadcast. Today the same crews, equipment and post-production pipelines make commercials, campaigns and social content for brands and institutions.

For brands

Ad films that sell

TV commercials, product launches, packaging reveals and social content that keeps going after launch, for consumer and industrial brands like Amway, Rathi Steel, Sohna Masala and Archita Masala.

For government & PSUs

Communication at national scale

Carefully fact-checked public campaigns, documentaries and programme films, with dubbing in several regional Indian languages and reach across the country.

For creators

You create. We build the rest.

A dedicated team that handles content planning, scripts, shoots, editing, channel management and label collaborations, so you can focus on your craft.

[ How it works ]

How a Palco production runs

The full pipeline runs under one roof, from pre-production through final colour grading and delivery, so nothing gets lost between vendors.

  2. 01

    The brief

    Send us the brief. A lead producer reviews it and replies with crew availability, location feasibility and an indicative cost band, usually within two working days.

  3. 02

    Pre-production

    Scriptwriting and creative development, treatment and storyboards, plus crew, kit and location management planned in-house.

  4. 03

    The shoot

    Cinema cameras and multi-unit location crews for studio, event and on-ground shoots, anywhere in India, from mega-plants to high-altitude border passes.

  5. 04

    Post-production

    Editing, colour grading, sound, voiceover, motion graphics and VFX, delivered as one finished cut.

  6. 05

    Delivery & distribution

    Masters that meet broadcast delivery specs for national TV, versions in regional languages, and platform-ready cuts for YouTube, Reels and Shorts.

[ FAQ ]

Questions, answered

What kind of projects does Palco Video take on?
Brand commercials and ad films, institutional and PSU film programmes, long-form series and features, plus ongoing social content operations for brands and creators.
Do you handle both production and marketing/distribution?
Yes. Palco has marketed 200+ Punjabi films and runs a channel with 1,70,000+ subscribers, so release and distribution sit in the same house as the shoot.
How quickly will I hear back after sending a brief?
A lead producer reviews every brief and replies with crew availability, location feasibility and an indicative cost band, usually within two working days.
How long does a typical commercial shoot take?
Most single-asset commercials need one to two shoot days, with post-production delivered within two to three weeks, depending on approvals and versioning.
What's included in a marketing management retainer?
Strategy, a monthly shoot calendar, editing and motion design, channel management, and campaign reporting.
Can you deliver in regional Indian languages?
Yes. Institutional and public-policy work routinely ships with dubbing in several regional Indian languages, alongside broadcast and digital versions.
Do you work outside Delhi, Chandigarh and Mumbai?
Yes. The NTPC programme alone covered eight plants across the country. Our three offices are a base, not a limit.
Can I pause or adjust a retainer plan?
Retainers are reviewed each quarter. Scope can scale up, scale down, or pause between campaign cycles.
