[ FAQ ]

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding our production timelines, retainers, and pan-India capabilities.

What kind of projects does Palco Video take on?
Brand commercials and ad films, institutional and PSU film programmes, long-form series and features, plus ongoing social content operations.
Do you handle both production and marketing/distribution?
Yes. Palco has marketed 200+ Punjabi films and runs a 1,70,000+ subscriber channel, so release and distribution sit in the same house as the shoot.
How long does a typical commercial shoot take?
Most single-asset commercials run one to two shoot days with post delivered inside two to three weeks, depending on approvals and versioning.
What's included in a marketing management retainer?
Strategy, a monthly shoot calendar, editing and motion design, channel management, and campaign reporting.
Do you work outside Delhi, Chandigarh and Mumbai?
Yes. The NTPC programme alone covered eight plants across the country. Three offices are the base, not the limit.
Can I pause or adjust a retainer plan?
Retainers are reviewed each quarter. Scope can scale up, scale down, or pause between campaign cycles.
