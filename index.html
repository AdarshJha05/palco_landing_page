What is Palco Video?

Palco Video makes video and gets it seen. We plan, shoot, edit and market commercials, campaigns and social content for brands, government and creators.

We are the brand-facing arm of a legacy production house. Ranjit Studio spent four decades making films, serials and Gurbani recordings for broadcast, and those same crews, equipment and post-production pipelines now work on your brief.

How a Palco production runs

Five stages, all in one house. Here is what happens between your brief and the finished film.

01 / 05 The brief

  2. 01

    The brief

    Send us the brief, no attachments needed at first. A lead producer replies with crew availability, location feasibility and an indicative cost band, usually within two working days.

  3. 02

    Pre-production

    Scriptwriting and creative development, plus crew, kit and location management.

  4. 03

    The shoot

    Cinema cameras and multi-unit location crews for studio, event and on-ground shoots, anywhere in India, from mega-plants to high-altitude border passes. A typical single-asset commercial takes one to two shoot days.

  5. 04

    Post-production

    Editing, colour grading, sound, voiceover, motion graphics and VFX. Post on a typical commercial is delivered within two to three weeks.

  6. 05

    Delivery & distribution

    Masters that meet broadcast delivery specs for national TV, versions in regional languages, and platform-ready cuts for YouTube, Reels and Shorts.

On a retainer? The same pipeline runs on a monthly content calendar, and scope is reviewed every quarter.

Questions, answered

What kind of projects does Palco Video take on?
Brand commercials and ad films, institutional and PSU film programmes, long-form series and features, plus ongoing social content operations for brands and creators.
Do you handle both production and marketing/distribution?
Yes. Palco has marketed 200+ Punjabi films and runs a channel with 1,70,000+ subscribers, so release and distribution sit in the same house as the shoot.
How quickly will I hear back after sending a brief?
A lead producer reviews every brief and replies with crew availability, location feasibility and an indicative cost band, usually within two working days.
How long does a typical commercial shoot take?
Most single-asset commercials need one to two shoot days, with post-production delivered within two to three weeks, depending on approvals and versioning.
What's included in a marketing management retainer?
Strategy, a monthly shoot calendar, editing and motion design, channel management, and campaign reporting.
Can you deliver in regional Indian languages?
Yes. Institutional and public-policy work routinely ships with dubbing in several regional Indian languages, alongside broadcast and digital versions.
Do you work outside Delhi, Chandigarh and Mumbai?
Yes. The NTPC programme alone covered eight plants across the country. Our three offices are a base, not a limit.
Can I pause or adjust a retainer plan?
Retainers are reviewed each quarter. Scope can scale up, scale down, or pause between campaign cycles.
